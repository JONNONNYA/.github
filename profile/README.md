## 🎮 About Us

**JONNONNYA** is where curiosity sparks an idea, and an idea becomes a game.

We create games that invite players to explore, take on challenges, team up with friends, and discover something of their own. We care about first impressions and the little details that turn an ordinary play session into a story worth telling.

## ✨ What Matters to Us

- **Gameplay with personality.** Clear rules, meaningful choices, and the joy of playing.
- **Worlds with atmosphere.** Visuals, sound, and details that work together.
- **Listening to players.** Shaping our games through feedback and real player experiences.
- **Quality in the details.** From responsive controls to intuitive interfaces.

## 🛠️ How We Create

We brainstorm, build prototypes, test ideas, and gradually shape them into complete game experiences.

Our core tools are **Roblox Studio**, **Luau**, and imagination.
